The database uses whole-second integer timing columns. The Miami chip and gun times are rounded to 1,543 and 1,581 seconds respectively; both original fractional times are preserved in the public profile notes and edition notes. The timing provider lists age 52, which conflicts with Roll's 1966 birth year. The published category is retained, but this result's age is deliberately omitted.

The two DNFs are labelled athlete supplied and link to his own published reports. Long Beach is a published archive result; the Miami result comes from the event's timing provider. Non-finishes have null times and places and do not enter personal bests or finish totals.

The current archive calls the event a 6K, but the field and winning time conflict with that distance. [American Trail Running Association history](https://trailrunner.com/event/malibu-creek-trail-challenge/) supplies a plausible event/date match, not proof of this runner's distance or identity. Do not publish as a verified 6K or assume 22K.

- **2009 Los Angeles Marathon, Wildflower, Vineman and Bulldog 50K:** mentioned as plans in the 2008 retrospective, not confirmed completed performances.
- **Chicago Marathon 2023:** a Richard Roll namesake result lacks enough identity evidence to attach to this profile.
- **41-mile run in October 2008:** [Own report](https://richroll.com/blog/40-mile-run-recap/) describes a self-directed training run, not an organised race. Likewise the [November training recap](https://richroll.com/blog/training-recap/) describes race simulation.
- **Historical Ultraman comparison:** [Year-by-year comparison](https://richroll.com/blog/ultraman-year-by-year-results-comparison/) retrospectively compares one performance with earlier fields; it does not document participation in those earlier editions.
