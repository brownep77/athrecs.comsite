# Aquatics link audit — 24 October 2026

Screened all 93 distinct primary website URLs used by the 236 directory organisations tagged swimming. The directory still contains 728 organisations. This includes cross-sport Olympic bodies and a shared membership reference; it is not a claim to have opened 236 separate federation homepages or validated every linked data page.

The accompanying `source-registry/aquatics-link-audit-2026-10-24.json` records each URL, retrieved title, observed destination, linked organisations and limitations. Its URLs are the pre-change audit inventory; the two regional homepage improvements below were separately opened afterwards.

## Findings and corrections

- Aquatics GB's homepage and About Us page identify the governing body and its three members: Swim England, Scottish Swimming and Swim Wales. Swim Ireland is a separate organisation. Existing directory wording already reflects this.
- Abbie Wood and Duncan Scott's matching biographies opened on aquaticsgb.com. Their two source registrations now use that domain. Previous britishswimming.org URLs and original check dates remain in source notes; stored athlete provenance is unchanged. These historical biographies do not establish current affiliations.
- Swim England East and South East directory website buttons now open the regional homepages. Their previously linked competition pages remain registered and retained as directory evidence.
- The 144 entries using World Aquatics' national-federation membership directory remain explicitly labelled membership references. Their direct fixture, result and athlete-profile sources are still research work.

## Retrieval scope and exceptions

Of the 93 primary URLs, 81 returned organisation-related content, one was the shared membership directory, seven returned only partial content, two need content review and two could not be retrieved. Content retrieval is not independent verification of every statement or of data availability.

Portugal and Brazil remained blocked or rate-limited through two retrieval methods. India and the Philippines initially timed out but returned their federation content through a second method. Scottish Swimming's domain is corroborated by Aquatics GB, while its own homepage and `/home` returned navigation-only content. Peru returned a loading message. Guatemala returned unavailable-media messages. Swiss, Finnish, Icelandic and Cypriot pages returned limited content; their detailed capabilities remain unassessed.

The Bulgarian page included placeholder text, and the West Midlands page included an unrelated casino article alongside its regional content. These are manual review flags, not claims that the organisations or domains are invalid. No replacement domains were guessed.

World Aquatics' separate results route loaded its sport filters but reported no results in the retrieved view. This confirms the route, not complete or currently available result data.

## Source additions and limits

Fourteen unrelated sources landed during this audit and are preserved. Twelve manual source registrations increase the registry from 841 to 853; all 36 enabled collectors remain unchanged, with 817 disabled sources. The additions are Aquatics GB governance, events, results overview, swimming/para-swimming/diving result archives, swimming/para-swimming/diving/artistic-swimming profile indexes, and the two regional homepages. Explicit organisation links retain the earlier regional competition sources after the homepage changes.

Swimming and para-swimming archive indexes visibly ended at 2024, with gaps; diving ended at 2023. Their coverage notes do not claim current completeness. Profile indexes expose named links and, where present, gender filters and pagination. Index discovery does not verify an athlete's nationality, biography or individual performance.

Ninety-six existing source records receive dated observations or URL changes while retaining their previous notes and check dates. No fixture dates, athlete facts, result rows or publication settings are changed by this audit. New and existing sources remain subject to page-specific and row-specific review.

Validation: source-registry schema and organisation coverage, disabled collector counts, TypeScript, focused lint and a development build. Private captures and before/after source records are kept in the staff audit; no athlete biography captures are committed or added to public profiles.
