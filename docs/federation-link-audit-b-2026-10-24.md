# Federation link audit B — 24 October 2026

Continues the previous aquatics review with all 65 direct triathlon organisation URLs in the directory, excluding three previously screened cross-sport Olympic bodies and membership-only references. All 11 earlier aquatics exceptions were revisited through their homepage or linked detail pages. The prior audit is retained unchanged; the accompanying JSON records this pass separately.

## Corrections

The listed Bermuda triathlon homepage returned unrelated football, NFL, tennis and basketball articles. Its public website button and reference now point to `https://www.bta.bm/`. The opened BTA homepage and About Us page identify the association, programmes and charity number; a historical organiser registration page also references bta.bm. World Triathlon's membership page still links the old domain, so that discrepancy is explicitly recorded rather than copied into the current source navigation. Previous URLs and check notes remain in the staff source audit. No claim is made about why the old site changed.

Vietnam's existing federation domain is corroborated by World Triathlon, but currently opens a Tam Chúc 2026 event sponsorship site. The public directory entry now explains that scope and directs readers to its existing World Triathlon reference for federation details. It is not described as a general fixture/results/profile index.

Only these two organisation records change; the total remains 728.

## Checks and limits

Of the 65 triathlon URLs, 57 returned organisation-related content, three were blocked, three returned partial content, one served unrelated content and one was a specific event landing page. The blocked pages were British Triathlon, Triathlon England and Triathlon Cymru; a second retrieval method returned HTTP 403. This is not evidence that their domains are wrong. Latvia, Cyprus and Israel returned limited content, so detailed data capabilities remain unconfirmed.

Useful deeper pages were opened for seven of the earlier aquatics exceptions: Bulgaria, Guatemala, Switzerland, Finland, Iceland, Cyprus and West Midlands. Those findings do not erase the original homepage limitations. Bulgaria's results archives and domestic calendar are usable research routes, but the calendar contains a postponed meet, a missing event name and a 2025 label in a 2026 table. Finland's calendar includes cancellation markers and pagination. Iceland's championship page explicitly warns that its draft session timetable may change. Cyprus links invitation, entry-list and result PDFs whose rows have not been verified.

Scottish Swimming, Portugal, Brazil and Peru still did not return substantive homepage content. Bulgaria's placeholder text and the unrelated West Midlands homepage article remain content-review warnings, even though separately inspected subpages provide useful material.

World Triathlon's event, result-filter and athlete-search routes opened with the expected titles and navigation, but no data rows were retrieved. Their notes distinguish a correct route from available results or athlete data.
