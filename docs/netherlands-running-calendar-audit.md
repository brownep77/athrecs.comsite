# Netherlands full running-calendar audit

Checked: 27 August 2026  
Calendar window: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027

## Result

The official Atletiekunie/Hardlopen.nl calendar was used as a discovery and reconciliation index. Its terms prohibit republishing its information without permission, so ATHRECS publishes only the subset that can be tied to an organiser-owned site or an authorised registration page:

- 1,147 official source listings reviewed
- 165 organiser-backed race series retained in this expansion
- 171 dated race occurrences retained
- 619 advertised distance rows retained
- 99 5K rows, 108 10K rows, 34 half-marathon rows and 20 marathon rows

Each advertised distance is stored separately so a same-day race card can show the full programme and distance filters remain accurate.

## Sources and boundary

The discovery backbone is the [Atletiekunie-powered Hardlopen.nl calendar](https://www.hardlopen.nl/evenementen/?datum-van=2026-01-01&datum-tot=2027-12-31). Its [disclaimer](https://www.hardlopen.nl/disclaimer/) says information may not be reused without prior permission. The snapshot was therefore used to find and reconcile events, while the public catalogue records point to organiser-owned or organiser-authorised registration sources. The snapshot was checked on 27 August 2026.

This is a public running-race calendar, not a copy of every athletics activity. It excludes:

- walks and walking-only distances
- training sessions, clinics, pre-runs and test runs
- pure relay/ekiden events
- standalone youth-only meetings
- cancelled events and cancelled distances
- parkruns already represented in ATHRECS' dedicated parkrun catalogue
- three Belgian border listings returned by the Dutch search
- events without an explicit date or usable advertised distance

No annual date was inferred. Events for which the only available evidence was the restricted federation page are documented as source gaps and are not republished. The smaller 2027 section reflects what organisers have actually announced and linked so far.

## Amsterdam correction

The federation entry lists only the half marathon and marathon, so the 2026 TCS Amsterdam Marathon programme was checked against the organiser's own pages. ATHRECS now includes:

- Saturday 17 October: 1K, 1.6K, 3.5K, 6.5K and 7.5K
- Sunday 18 October: 1K Mini Marathons, half marathon and marathon
- Sunday 31 October 2027: half marathon and marathon, the distances currently advertised in the official Dutch calendar

The Amsterdam City Walk is deliberately excluded because it is a walking event.

## Data-quality corrections

- Source distances expressed in metres (for example `21100`) are normalised to kilometres.
- Duplicate event-detail pages and same-day programme fragments are merged before publication.
- Drenthe Loopfestijn's cancelled marathon is excluded while its other races remain.
- Diepe Hel Holterbergloop's Saturday 6K and Sunday 10K/half marathon are assigned to their advertised programme dates.
- Future entry links appear only when a live organiser or registration URL was present at the check date.
