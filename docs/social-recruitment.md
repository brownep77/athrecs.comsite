# Social recruitment

Campaign destination: https://www.athrecs.com/join

Use `?from=instagram`, `facebook`, `tiktok`, `x`, `linkedin`, `youtube` or `strava` to name the originating platform. Unknown values are discarded. These values customise the copy; they do not verify a follow or record campaign conversions.

The journey is follow reminder → email-code sign-in → name and sport → saved private profile → add results and details. Following is optional. The follow button is a self-reported continuation, not an API check. Existing profiles are taken to the completion links without overwriting their data. Privacy acknowledgement is explicit; marketing preferences and sharing remain unchanged.

No official ATHRECS social account URLs were found in the current site configuration. The first step therefore asks visitors to follow the account on the social post/profile that brought them here. Add direct follow links only after the account URLs are confirmed by the owner. No social posts or direct messages are sent by this feature.

Suggested post or bio call to action: Follow ATHRECS, then create your athlete profile at athrecs.com/join. Your results. Your achievements. Your sporting story.
