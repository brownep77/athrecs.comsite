## Denmark

### Copenhagen Marathon

- [Official homepage](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/): confirms 9 May 2027 and
  09:30. The page labels this `CET`, although the race is in May. The guide keeps
  the published local wall-clock time and uses `Europe/Copenhagen`; it does not
  convert the organiser's abbreviation into a fixed UTC offset.
- [Course](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/the-course/): explicitly describes
  2026, including the flat city course and refreshment zones. The guide labels
  this a preview and asks runners to check the 2027 replacement.
- [Elite information](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/my-race/elite/): separate
  Elite A selection and Elite B performance evidence. Men: marathon 2:25:00,
  half marathon 1:08:30 or 10 km 31:00. Women: marathon 2:50:00, half marathon
  1:20:00 or 10 km 36:00.
- The same elite page gives **25 April 2027** in the Elite B section and
  **24 April 2027** in the competition terms as the end of the qualifying
  performance window. This is a qualifying-window conflict, not a verified
  application deadline. The guide retains the discrepancy and directs athletes
  to Sparta for confirmation.
- [Charity entries](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/run-the-copenhagen-marathon-and-support-a-good-cause-along-the-way/)
  and [official travel operators](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/official-tour-operators-for-copenhagen-marathon/):
  authorised alternative entry routes, with fundraising or package conditions.
- [Results archive](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/my-race/resultatarkiv/) and
  [media page](https://copenhagenmarathon.dk/en/media/): official archive and
  organiser-linked 2026 result discovery. The media page links Ultimate. No timer
  result rows or individual performances were compared or imported.
- The homepage also advertised European Marathon Classics. The integrating
  series work was informed; this module does not invent series rules.

### HCA Marathon, Odense

- [Official homepage](https://hcamarathon.dk/): confirms the 27th edition on
  26 September 2027, links the 2027 SportsTiming entry and explicitly says the
  2027 programme will follow.
- [Course page](https://hcamarathon.dk/ruten/): labelled 2026; describes a
  half-length circuit used for two marathon laps, an athletics-stadium finish
  and a 21-metre difference between the highest and lowest points. That figure
  is **not total ascent**.
- The checked site mixes the confirmed 2027 date with older programme, course,
  fast-runner, transport and road-closure material. The guide does not promote
  the old 10:00 start or six-hour traffic-free window into a final 2027 timetable.
- Collection and race services are based at Odense Idrætspark; final 2027 hours,
  cutoffs, fast-runner requirements and pacers remain subject to the organiser's
  edition-specific instructions. No unconfirmed entry fee is hardcoded.
- The organiser's actual 2026 results button links
  [SportsTiming event 16156](https://www.sportstiming.dk/event/16156/results).
  Opening the timer destination returned a **human-verification wall**. The
  organiser link was inspected, but no timing table or athlete row was obtained.
  HCA remains the organiser and SportsTiming the linked timing provider.

### Copenhagen Half Marathon

- [Official homepage](https://cphhalf.dk/en/) and
  [2027 race page](https://cphhalf.dk/en/the-race/): 19 September 2027, 09:30 on
  Jagtvej, 21.0975 km, SuperHalfs membership and announced capacity of 45,000.
  Capacity is not reported as a count of finishers.
- The ordinary **2027 CPH Half** is kept separate from the **2026 World Athletics
  Road Running Championships**. Dates, start arrangements and entry processes
  are not transferred between them.
- [FAQ](https://cphhalf.dk/en/faq/): the 2027 lottery draw was completed on
  9 October 2026. The checked terms do not permit resale or transfer; the guide
  does not suggest an unofficial secondary market.
- [Terms and conditions](https://cphhalf.dk/en/terms-and-conditions/): the stated
  three-hour limit is **net time**, measured from crossing the start line.
- The race page says 2027 Elite B/C standards and their qualifying period will
  be published later. No Copenhagen Marathon standards or old half-marathon
  thresholds are substituted. Expo location and hours also await publication.
- [Results archive](https://cphhalf.dk/en/results-archive/): annual archive links,
  including 2025. No individual result rows were compared or imported.
- Existing SuperHalfs content was cross-checked for consistency; it was not
  edited by this country-guide work.

### Aarhus City Half

- [Official homepage](https://www.aarhuscityhalf.dk/en/): confirms 20 June 2027
  and links Aarhus Motion registration.
- [Race-day information](https://www.aarhuscityhalf.dk/en/loebet-loebsdagen/):
  contains a 2027 map heading and collection dates alongside 2026 wording and

  does not treat the displayed 10:00 timetable as an unqualified 2027 start.

  subject to final edition instructions.
- [Participation page](https://www.aarhuscityhalf.dk/en/deltag/): organiser resale

- The linked result reference is a **past-winners archive**, covering 2012–2026,
  rather than a complete table of finishers. No participant performances were
  imported and no uninspected timing-provider identifier was constructed.

## Result-source recovery and validation scope

The original source-registry work checked the CSV and wider event inventory before
adding disabled manual references. Sparta's two Copenhagen archives shared one
new record; HCA, SportsTiming Denmark, Aarhus winners, Třeboň and Stopnito were
separately identified. Existing RunCzech was reused.

The recovered source patch is supplied separately as
`/tmp/athrecs-source-registry-additions.json`: 14 disabled proposed rows covering
the five-country expansion and four evidence-note additions for existing source
IDs. The recovered patch was deduplicated and merged into main at `f2c7ef8` on
24 October 2026: all 827 existing records were retained, 14 disabled records were
added and four existing notes received dated additions. The resulting 841-source
registry retains 36 enabled collectors. Source and bulk-run verifiers passed.

Registration does not enable automated collection or verify a participant result.
The exact SportsTiming human-verification limitation, SportHive JavaScript-shell
observations, Porto provisional status and Berlin private-use restriction remain
in the recovered source notes. No participant details are included.

The eight original records passed an import/shape check before the scratch loss.
The recovery import/shape check also passed: four modules, eight unique slugs,
eight expected edition dates, 119 HTTPS links and the preserved source-check
date. The separate JSON patch passed checks for 14 disabled rows, four existing
source-note additions and its 24-column source schema. These checks do not verify
every linked site's availability or merge the newer registry. Full TypeScript,
route, SEO and production-build verification belong to the integrating release;
the earlier SSR attempt received no HTTP response and must not be reported as a pass.
