comes from the organiser programme, not from matching race titles. The current
three-month window is 26 October 2026–26 January 2027. Seven retained distance
options fall within it, all with published starts and appropriate wave/pen scope;
one further option is beyond it and seven are historical. This is a batch review,
not a claim that the catalogue-wide start queue is complete.
