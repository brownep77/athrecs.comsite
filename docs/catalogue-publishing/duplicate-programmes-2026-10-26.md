# Reviewed duplicate fixture programmes — 26 October 2026

Owner request: Chase the Moon shows its 10K twice; retain the listing with both distances and apply the same check throughout fixtures.

## Scope and outcome

Screened 6,088 non-parkrun event records containing 11,866 dated distance rows from 1 October 2026 onwards in the current production database. All available countries, sports and future months were included. Recurring parkruns are outside this multi-distance programme pass. Automated name/source comparisons produced 399 candidate date-pairs; 54 involved a multi-distance programme and relevant name similarity. A second pass normalised UK country labels and month names, producing 410 candidate pairs and 13 additional relevant multi-distance matches. A final token-subset pass inspected 14 further pairs, including short Tatton names. These are research candidates, not verified duplicates.

20 redundant event/date listings are suppressed in favour of 17 existing complete programmes. This removes 28 redundant edition rows from public fixture projections while preserving every distinct reviewed distance. Original events, editions, entry links, results and other dependants remain unchanged in the database. Other dates attached to the same event remain public. The existing shared date-review filter applies to the homepage, sport pages, searches, race details, location pages and calendar exports.
