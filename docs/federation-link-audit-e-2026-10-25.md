# Federation link audit E — 25 October 2026

Reviewed the 61 remaining athletics organisations whose primary website was a membership-directory reference after audit D. These entries share 29 URLs: 33 Asian members use one Asian Athletics directory, 24 African members have individual CAA profiles, and four American/Caribbean bodies use Olympic or continental references. Each reference was opened and its federation entries checked.

## Changes

- Upgraded 12 directory entries to corroborated national websites: Bahrain, Chinese Taipei, Iraq, Kazakhstan, Lebanon, Maldives, Myanmar, Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Uzbekistan, Morocco and Namibia. Original membership references remain linked.
- Added 49 manual source references and reviewed 31 existing source rows. The registry now has 1,065 sources: 36 enabled and 1,029 disabled. All 740 directory organisations remain registered.
- Corrected the existing Morocco source from the unsupported, unreachable `frme.ma` destination to `frma.ma`, explicitly linked by Morocco’s National Olympic Committee. The original source ID, notes and private before/after evidence are retained.
- Removed unsupported 2026–2027 fixture-coverage years from the existing Qatar source; its annual planning matrix does not establish confirmed fixtures.
- Inspected 23 national-site candidates, 35 specific research destinations and Namibia’s Olympic member index. The machine-readable companion distinguishes page-content evidence from inferred capabilities.

## Findings and qualifications

Maldives has a launch notice on its homepage but working events and results links. The national championship results include individual tables credited to MeetPro by DirectAthletics; conflicting AM/PM labels in the meet schedule require clarification before any start-time use. Lebanon’s calendar contains dates and results references, while its generic results sections returned no rows. A linked half-marathon PDF contains a result table but no nationality or confirmed chip/gun field.

Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan provide named 2026 results-document indexes. Their homepage/calendar content also includes older years. Chinese Taipei mixes ROC-year and Gregorian labels; Iran uses the Iranian calendar. Ethiopia’s candidate results site uses Ethiopian-calendar years: these were not converted or treated as Gregorian dates.

Bahrain explicitly marks the West Asia Athletics Championships as postponed until further notice. Qatar’s competitions page includes indicative plans, and its performance page explains development pathways rather than athlete biographies. UAE’s athlete index returned filters with zero athletes; country dropdown choices are not nationality evidence. Several Namibia, Morocco, Lebanon, Kazakhstan and Ethiopia sections returned descriptions, navigation or forms without the expected records. These remain qualified manual review destinations.

Bahamas’ membership-linked news website contains placeholder About text, so its Olympic reference remains primary. The linked Dominican site showed a service-unavailable notice. Bhutan, Cambodia, China, Tajikistan, Vietnam and an Algerian candidate could not be opened successfully during this pass; their membership references remain. Iran and Palestine’s linked HTTP websites opened, but HTTPS failed through both retrieval methods. Their public HTTPS membership references remain; the HTTP sites are retained for manual research. Ethiopia’s self-identifying website is registered as a research lead pending independent domain corroboration.

No fixtures, start times, athlete profiles, nationalities or individual performances were imported, corrected or marked verified. Homepage and document inspection does not establish participant identity or collection rights.

## Validation

Source-registry, organisation-coverage, bulk-source, directory, type, focused lint and development-build checks passed. Preservation checks compared all previous organisations and registry rows, retained original notes and associations, and confirmed the 36 enabled collectors remain unchanged. Private source captures and before/after evidence are retained in the staff audit.
