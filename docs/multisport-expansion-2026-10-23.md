# Multisport expansion — 23 October 2026

This continuation adds reviewed source records and national federation entries. It does not independently verify the whole athlete or fixture catalogue.

## Results and athlete facts

- 123 additional completed triathlon performances extend 59 existing unclaimed profiles. Eight elite programmes across Samarkand, Quiberon, Alghero, Yokohama, Tarragona and Chengdu were compared using programme JSON, programme XLSX and the athletes' same-day captured history XLSX files.
- Five new unclaimed athlete profiles carry nine additional completed performances, five explicit sporting nationalities and five explicitly reported birth years. Exact birthdays, residence, birthplace and representative remain unknown. Their source profile IDs agree with programme IDs and athlete history exports.
- Nine DNF observations are held outside completed-result totals. The official programme JSON reports DNF while its spreadsheet uses zero total time; zero is never imported as a finish.
- Existing names, provider IDs and possible surname matches were screened. A further alias check found one possible existing athlete among six shortlisted candidates; that profile and its performance were held. No uncertain identity was merged.
- Existing privacy, ownership, nationality, profile facts and original performances are preserved. Canonical programme result mirrors are excluded from duplicate profile display. Source verification does not establish account ownership.

Original official source captures and the reviewed before/after values remain in the staff-only evidence and audit tables. Previously archived same-day history captures are referenced with their original capture date; this pass does not claim a second fresh fetch of those files.

## Fixtures

44 new 2027 road-cycling programme fixtures cover 27 men's and 17 women's elite events. Forty-three were compared with their current UCI competition-detail pages; the women's Vuelta dates are confirmed by the UCI's September Management Committee announcement. Known Belgian event series were excluded from new-event creation, and repeated calendar rows were deduplicated.

The current calendar and September decision establish Eschborn–Frankfurt on 1 May, superseding the June announcement of 2 May. Vuelta a Burgos Feminas remains held: the September announcement says 6–10 May, while the current competition page and calendar say 6–9 May. The Tour de France Femmes is deferred pending review of the new organiser route announcement. Start times, race distances, individual stage schedules and entry availability remain unspecified.

A calendar country identifies the governing body's programme listing; it does not imply that every stage of an international tour takes place in that country.

## Federation directory

24 national athletics and cycling bodies are added for Bahrain, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Maldives, Mongolia, Pakistan and the United Arab Emirates. Membership is checked against the current Asian Athletics and UCI Asian member directories. Links to member directories are explicitly labelled as such; no unsupported national website or contact details are invented.

The directory now has 373 organisations, including 325 national federations, across 87 countries and territories. All eleven sport sections remain accessible. This batch adds athletics and cycling membership coverage; it does not assert that every cycling body has independently verified coverage of all cycling disciplines.
