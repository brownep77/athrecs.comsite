# Directory expansion: Caribbean, Mediterranean, Mauritius and Uruguay

Reviewed on 23 October 2026. Adds 32 national federations, taking the directory
from 261 to 293 organisations, including 245 national bodies in 61 countries and
territories. The 261 earlier records, including their review dates, are unchanged.
The record helpers now accept a review-date argument so new research does not
silently redate older entries.

Every country also has swimming and track-and-field coverage. Road running is
included for Jamaica, Cyprus, Malta, Mauritius and Uruguay. The other three
athletics records remain track-and-field-only in this review. These mappings
describe evidence reviewed for the directory, not the complete possible remit
of each federation. Trail running, BMX and duathlon are not inferred simply from
membership of an international federation.

## Identity and scope evidence

World Triathlon's current member profiles identify all eight affiliated triathlon
bodies. World Aquatics' national-federation directory identifies the swimming
bodies. NACAC identifies the four Caribbean athletics organisations; national
sites, Balkan Athletics, SportMalta and the Uruguayan Olympic Committee support
the other athletics entries. Cycling identity checks use COPACI, UEC, the UCI,
Olympic committee profiles and national federation sites.

Jamaica's cycling site lists national road and track championships. Trinidad and
Tobago's federation explicitly identifies road, track and mountain biking, with
BMX described as a future expansion rather than a current discipline. Cyprus'
official competition notices cover road and mountain biking. Uruguay's federation
lists all four directory cycling disciplines. UCI's indexed official national
championship entries support Malta's road and Mauritius' road/mountain-bike
coverage; direct retrieval of those dynamic pages sometimes returned only the
navigation shell. Barbados Police Service's official June 2026 traffic notice
establishes the Cycling Union's road programme. The Bahamas Cycling Federation's
2026 national road programme is additionally corroborated by The Tribune's
2 June 2026 report quoting the federation president; that is a supplemental press
source, separate from the official identity sources.

Duathlon is supported by Barbados' championship page, Trinidad and Tobago's
October 2026 school-series calendar, Cyprus' explicit national remit, Malta's
national championship history, and Mauritius' current federation description.
The remaining three entries are triathlon-only in this batch.

## Destination findings

- Jamaica athletics uses `athleticsja.org`, with current JAAA membership notices
  and competition information. NACAC's older `trackandfieldjm.com` address was
  blocked on retrieval. Jamaica aquatics uses `asaj.com.jm`; World Aquatics still
  lists the separate SwimJamaica programme site.
- Bahamas Aquatics uses `bahamasaquatics.com`, confirmed by the Bahamas Olympic
  Committee. The older swimming-federation domain was unavailable.
- Trinidad and Tobago aquatics uses `ttaquatics.com`, whose about page identifies
  ASATT. World Aquatics' older `asatt.8m.com` address is not used. Athletics uses
  the current `naaatt.org` site and its own full organisation name.
- Mauritius swimming uses `mauritiusaquatics.org` and displays Mauritius Swimming
  Federation. The French name retained by World Aquatics remains a searchable alias.
- Uruguay cycling uses `federacionciclista.com.uy`, whose current licensing page
  establishes its national role. COPACI still lists an older domain.
- The address listed for Uruguay triathlon, `triatlonuruguay.org`, currently
  presents Unión de Triatlón Costa Azul, a different amateur organisation. It is
  excluded as a public destination and as evidence for the national body's duathlon
  scope. The official World Triathlon profile is used instead.
- Eight new entries use labelled member-directory destinations: Jamaica triathlon;
  Barbados cycling and swimming; Bahamas athletics and cycling; Malta cycling;
  Mauritius cycling; Uruguay triathlon. These are World Triathlon, World Aquatics,
  Olympic committee or UCI entries, not substitute national websites. Structured
  data omits the parent directory as the federation's own URL or `sameAs`.
- Jamaica triathlon's member profile lists a social group. Barbados cycling,
  Barbados swimming, Bahamas cycling and Malta cycling had unavailable standalone
  destinations. A standalone Mauritius cycling website was not established.
  Bahamas athletics uses the Olympic Committee's confirmed federation contact
  listing; its linked sports-news site was not independently established as a
  usable federation-owned destination in this review.
- Mauritius triathlon and Uruguay athletics blocked one automated retrieval method
  but their current official content was accessible through web retrieval. Their
  own websites are retained. A blocked request alone is not treated as a dead site.

## Validation

The directory verifier covers the eight new country filters, sport-specific
inclusions/exclusions, current names and aliases, review dates and official-directory
destinations. Structural comparison confirms all 261 existing records are unchanged.
Type checking and the development build cover integration without local database
migrations or catalogue publication. Live verification covers all 32 new profiles,
country and sport filters, member-directory labels and structured data, existing
Northern Ireland remit and sitemap inclusion.

This is a researched directory expansion, not a complete global census or a fresh
audit of every older record. No athlete profiles or results are imported.
