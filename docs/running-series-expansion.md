# Running series guide expansion — 23 October 2026

## Scope

`src/data/running-series-guides.ts` adds eight editorial guides for the public
running-guides hub. These records describe race families, completion challenges
and championships separately. They do not add fixture editions, import athlete
results, register new data collectors or claim search-engine indexing.

The check date is **2026-10-23**. Advice about choosing races, travel, equipment
organisation and keeping result evidence is AthRecs editorial guidance. Format,
membership, completion and ranking facts are based on the official sources below.
No 2027 dates, prices, entry availability or start times are inferred from earlier
editions. No physiological or medical prescription is included.

Recovery note, 24 October 2026: the original scratch checkout was lost before

## European Marathon Classics

Sources opened and inspected:

- https://europeanmarathonclassics.eu/ — the eight named member marathons.
- https://europeanmarathonclassics.eu/the-classics-challenge/ — current FAQ,
  entry separation, completion, e-coins, historical results and medal collection.
- https://europeanmarathonclassics.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/EMC-Rules-and-Regulations.pdf
  — official regulations, particularly sections 2, 3 and the completion provisions.
- https://europeanmarathonclassics.eu/european-marathon-classics-unlocks-marathon-history-today/
  — historical-result verification launched 15 July 2026; account workflow.

Recorded distinctions: five different member marathons/cities are sufficient;
there are eight member events; there is no final deadline or prescribed order;
registration for the challenge is free and separate from individual paid race
entry. Historical results count only after EMC verification. The current FAQ says
digital e-coins are available and physical coins are not distributed at individual
finishes; it separately describes in-person medal collection. The guide therefore
does not promise a free physical coin at every finish or postal medal delivery.

ITRA National League was initially researched but was replaced by this more
directly relevant marathon-completion challenge. No ITRA guide is published in
this batch.

## Marathon des Sables

Sources opened and inspected:

- https://marathondessables.com/en/les-formats-du-marathon-des-sables — official
  comparison of seven formats, published 12 March 2026.
- https://marathondessables.com/en/format/mds-legendary — approximate 250km,
  six stages, Moroccan Sahara, food self-sufficiency and variable roadbook.
- https://marathondessables.com/fr/format/mds-120 — the official MDS 120 format
  destination reached through the English comparison page's own link.

Recorded distinctions: Legendary and MDS 120 are different formats; MDS 120 has
70/100/120km options across three stages; other MDS formats have different support
and staging. Exact prior stage lengths, prices, insurance promises and future
entry status are deliberately omitted. The guide is an event-family guide,
not an invented passport or whole-family completion scheme.

## RacingThePlanet / 4 Deserts

Sources opened and inspected:

- https://www.racingtheplanet.com/frequently-asked-questions — seven-day,
  six-stage, 250km format; carried supplies; organiser support; qualifying entry
  for The Last Desert; separate one-day Antarctica Ultramarathon.
- https://www.racingtheplanet.com/4-deserts-club — accepted four-desert set and
  required full-event completions.
- https://www.racingtheplanet.com/4-deserts-grand-slam — Grand Slam concept and
  the explicitly published 2028 schedule.
- https://www.racingtheplanet.com/4-deserts-grand-slam-plus — fifth designated
  travelling event, reached through the official page's redirect.
- https://www.racingtheplanet.com/entry-details — entry packages, team categories,
  calendar and the one-year challenge description.

Recorded distinctions: Namib/Namibia, Gobi/Mongolia, Atacama/Chile and The Last
Desert/Antarctica form the current set. The Last Desert requires at least two
accepted prior RacingThePlanet or 4 Deserts ultras under the published FAQ. Club
completion, Grand Slam and Grand Slam Plus are distinct. No 2027 four-race Grand
Slam is inferred; the currently displayed complete programme is 2028. Antarctica
is labelled as a destination without inventing a sovereign country or flag.

## Integration notes

- Use `/running/series/{slug}` for canonical editorial routes.
- `kind` is `series` for completion challenges/championships and `collection` for
  event families; `format` provides the clearer runner-facing distinction.
- Lowercase `faqs` contains visible question-and-answer content. No promise of
  FAQ rich results, voice selection or AI citation is made.
- `countries` includes a two-letter `code` only for actual named countries;
  international calendars, variable destinations and Antarctica are plain text.
- `relatedCountryIds` is deliberately limited to existing/new guide countries
  with relevant coverage. It is not a complete series-membership declaration.
- Keep full editorial records out of the directory's compact client payload.
- Preserve all existing fixture, athlete, result and source-publication controls.
