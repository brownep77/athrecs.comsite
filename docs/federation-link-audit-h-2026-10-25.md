Reviewed 14 targeted African national cycling entries and added nine Moroccan regional cycling leagues. This is not a fresh verification of every African federation: the shared UCI page returned navigation without its member rows, and direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. Existing UCI references and all 52 of their organisation associations are preserved.

## Source findings

Namibia's national site is explicitly linked by its Olympic committee. Its calendar and Results route returned incomplete dynamic content, while search-index snapshots showed different months. A championship news article supplies podium categories but no times, with spelling/encoding and publication-date inconsistencies. Neither publication dates nor podium summaries establish complete individual performances.

Benin has competition and national-team reports. Narrative sprint classifications, race gaps and neutralised departures must remain separate from final results and actual starts. One report combines a weekday with an incompatible calendar date and has an inconsistent classification order; no dates or results were imported from it.

Morocco's calendar route returned no fixture rows, but its dated bulletin index links substantive documents. Bulletin 17 is an eight-page PDF; text was extracted and page 7 visually checked. It distinguishes control from departure for the 18 October 2026 Oued Laou circuit. Other pages contain licensing and club information, which is not a performance dataset.

Ghana's programme portal contains a real PDF iframe despite placeholder fallback text. The one-page 2026 plan was downloaded and visually checked. Its month-only entries mix competitions, camps, administration and ceremonies. Its footer carries 20251208; the upload path is not a revision date. An Africa Road Championships entry names November and Ethiopia, conflicting with the opened 2026 Kisumu programme. Treat this as an older planning lead and reconcile editions before importing.

Zambia's event archive contains dated national championship notices, including category-specific distances. Some template text and old events labelled upcoming remain. The 2026 road championship page's lower generic distance summary differs from its category-specific description; no category or start was inferred.

Cameroon's site links a seven-page Kisumu results bulletin. Text was extracted and PDF page 3 visually inspected. The document credits **ChronoRace** for timing/results and supplies UCI ID, bib, nationality code, country, placing and Time / Gap columns, with DNS, DNF and OTL. It is a report dated 24 October, not proof of the entire championship's final results. Combined elite/U23 results and separate classifications must remain distinct. Gaps are not finish durations; blank cells are not zero. Nationality is source-reported information and still needs identity matching before attachment to a profile.

The African Road Championships website and schedule are registered as edition-specific sources, not as the continental confederation's homepage. Their elite/U23 ITT distances disagree. The exact organiser-linked 2026 ACN Timing route returned empty dynamic content and remains a qualified manual lead. The programme distinguishes competitions, a social ride and a ceremony; event-specific roadbooks still need comparison before any fixture publication.

## Held or limited destinations

Rwanda's domain is corroborated by the Olympic committee directory, but its About page contains equestrian-template personnel and several event titles share an ambiguous 2025 label. Those names and labels were not accepted as athlete, leadership or race-date facts. Uganda's national domain is under maintenance. Zimbabwe's researched domain returned a coming-soon placeholder through the web fallback. Botswana's specific commission profile contains historical 2009 information.

Algeria's candidate national site and Eswatini's sports-council profile remained inaccessible after two retrieval methods. Cycling South Africa's existing homepage returned HTTP 403 through both methods. Existing references are retained; a retrieval failure is not proof of an incorrect domain. A similarly named Eswatini cycling blog was not mistaken for the governing association.

No fixtures, starts, athlete records, nationalities or individual performances were imported, corrected or marked verified. Registration does not activate collection. Original dates, notes and references are retained; raw captures and individual-source samples remain in the private audit.
