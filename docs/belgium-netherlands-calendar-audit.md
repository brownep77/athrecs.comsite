# Belgium and Netherlands running-calendar audit

Checked: 27 August 2026  
Coverage: completed 2026 public races and explicitly advertised fixtures through 31 December 2027.

## Publication boundary

- Adult/open timed running races on road and trail are included.
- Every explicitly advertised distance is stored as a separate edition row.
- Walks, youth-only races, relays and training runs are excluded.
- No annual date is inferred. A 2027 row exists only where the organiser currently publishes that edition.
- Existing ATHRECS, World Athletics, AIMS and marathon-directory identities are reconciled before publication.

## Main discovery sources

Belgium was checked through organiser pages, the EnergyVision Running Tour, Keep on Running, Atletiek Vlaanderen, official municipal pages and French-language organiser records. The federation calendars were used for discovery or verification only where their bulk-reuse status is not cleared.

The Netherlands was checked through organiser pages, Le Champion, Golazo event pages and Atletiekunie/Hardlopen.nl records. The national calendar describes roughly 2,200 events per year, so this release deliberately applies the public adult timed-race boundary rather than copying youth meetings, walks or informal club sessions.

## Material corrections

- Knokke is published as 6K, 10K and half marathon from the municipality's current information.
- Dwars door Brugge is published as 5K and 15K from the city information.
- Athora Bruges' 8K family walk is excluded.
- Singelloop Utrecht is published as 5K and 10K only; no half marathon is advertised.
- Marathon Amersfoort's training marathon is excluded.
- Schoorl's incorrect legacy marathon row is retired in favour of 10K, half marathon and 30K.
- Generic World Athletics rows for Deloitte Fast & Fun, Dam tot Damloop and Marathon The Hague are replaced with their advertised distances.

## Result

- Belgium: 33 race series, 37 race dates, 97 advertised-distance rows.
- Netherlands: 32 race series, 48 race dates, 115 advertised-distance rows.
- Combined: 65 race series, 85 race dates, 212 advertised-distance rows.
