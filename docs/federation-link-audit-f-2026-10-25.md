# Federation link audit F — 25 October 2026

Reviewed all 144 swimming organisations whose primary link still pointed to World Aquatics' national-federation directory, and revisited Scottish Swimming's results section. Matched each federation against the actual membership page, distinguishing swimming bodies from separately listed water-polo or diving bodies. Of those 144 entries, 62 had linked national websites and 82 listed no website; this does not establish that those 82 organisations have no website elsewhere.

## Changes

- Upgraded 21 public primary links to corroborated federation homepages: Morocco, Namibia, Fiji, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, Vanuatu, Antigua and Barbuda, Puerto Rico, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Iran, Maldives, Mongolia, Pakistan, Andorra, Azerbaijan, Faroe Islands, Liechtenstein, Russia, San Marino, Ukraine and Albania.
- Updated Scottish Swimming's results guidance and added Czechia's working competition portal while retaining its membership reference as the primary link. All original references remain linked.
- Added 60 disabled manual research sources and reviewed two existing shared/homepage rows. The registry now contains 1,149 sources: 36 enabled and 1,113 disabled. All 740 organisations remain associated with registered sources; all 144 reviewed memberships remain explicitly associated with World Aquatics.
- Recorded 35 deeper-page/document assessments alongside 25 qualified national website references. A route that returned navigation or placeholders is labelled accordingly, not described as a working data feed.

## Aquatics findings

Fiji's old federation domain redirects to Fiji Aquatics; Liechtenstein's redirects to Liechtenstein Swimming. Fiji's 2026 calendar contains competitions alongside meetings, camps and holidays and is subject to change. Andorra links 2026–27 calendars and records and 2025–26 results. Ukraine has a dated 2026 results index, while its team section includes qualification standards: these are not achieved performances. Russia's competition index exposes dates and result links, but incomplete event-name extraction requires edition-specific follow-up.

Guam's January 24, 2026 results label points to a filename containing 2025; the edition discrepancy must be resolved before import. Morocco's opened results section covers historical 2015–19 material and Namibia's covers 2020–21. San Marino's archive URL currently describes the May 2026 Meeting Squalo Blu. Maldives' record table contains years, not exact event dates. Belarus' records table has a competition-country field, which must not become athlete nationality.

Scottish Swimming's interactive results hub exposed its 2026 document links. Three PDFs were downloaded successfully: SNAGS (119 pages), National Open Friday (25 pages) and Summer Meet (95 pages). Their first pages were inspected for column headings and table structure. They contain names, ages, clubs, times and split distances; no nationality column was found. Scottish Swimming is the publisher. OCS-Software's website footer credit does not establish a timing provider. No individual identities or performances were compared, and the calendar and athlete-profile sections remain unassessed.

Czechia's main federation homepage could not be retrieved, but its official competition subdomain opened successfully. A linked Znojmo programme distinguishes session starts from warm-up times and provides PDF/Lenex results-document links; the result files themselves were not inspected.

## Retained limitations

The 62 national websites produced 36 retrievable responses and 26 failures, all retried with a second retrieval method. A retrieval failure is not proof of an incorrect domain. Of the 36 responses, 21 supported homepage upgrades, four remain qualified historical/HTTP research references, and 11 require further review because of social redirects, maintenance/loading pages, parking, server listings or unsuitable content.

Aruba and Zimbabwe expose old material. Belarus redirects to HTTP and North Macedonia's HTTP page contains 2023 material; their public HTTPS membership references remain. Tanzania and Kosovo display maintenance/construction notices, and Uganda exposes a loading shell. Saint Lucia and UAE have parking-style destinations; UAE's tokenized redirect is retained only in private evidence. Lebanon redirects to a web-design service notice. Marshall Islands' destination is a generic directory, Uzbekistan shows a server directory, Papua New Guinea has a conflicting location in its page title, and Monaco's destination includes unrelated casino promotion. None was promoted to an operational federation homepage.

No fixtures, start times, athlete-profile facts, nationalities or individual performances were imported, corrected or marked verified. General eligibility language, club affiliation, competition location, age and birth year do not establish nationality or an exact birth date. Collection remains disabled for every new source pending rights and data review.
