# UK and Ireland 10K follow-up

Checked: 22 August 2026

Horizon: 22 August 2026 to 31 December 2027

Scope: road, trail and mixed-surface 10K races in England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland.

The release also adds 10K discoverability to existing, already-dated cards for Run Balmoral, the Maypole Project Chislehurst Half Marathon and the Stansted House Trail Run. This avoids duplicate event cards.

## Duplicates excluded

The audit did not republish races already represented through their series distance list or an existing 10K edition. Examples include Sandymount Night Run, Edinburgh Marathon Festival, ABP Newport Marathon Festival, Gloucester Half Marathon & 10K, Leeds Running Festival, Wirral Running Festival, Cheltenham Running Festival, Out Of The Blue Battle and Oliver Fisher 10K & 5K.

## Held candidates

Nenagh Half Marathon & 10K, RunClare Eamon Moloney Ennis 10K and Waterford Viking Marathon 10K remain outside the public catalogue because their Athletics Ireland permits are pending. Portadown Running Festival is also held because Athletics Northern Ireland confirms its date but does not publish a 10K distance.
