## Primary evidence and sport scope

Each record contains the specific official references used. World Triathlon's
current member profiles establish all eight triathlon affiliations. World
Aquatics' national-federation directory establishes all eight aquatic members.
Asian Athletics and the current Atletismo Sudamericano `.com` directory identify
the athletics federations. Cycling evidence uses national-federation pages,
Olympic committee profiles, UCI entries and Hong Kong government guidance.

Argentina's UCRA September 2026 report and FACIMO's institutional page establish
the three-member cycling structure. FACPyR is limited to road and track; FACIMO
to mountain biking; FAB to BMX. UCRA covers the combined scope. Related links
connect the umbrella and discipline bodies, without presenting them as duplicate
organisations. Chile's technical structure, Colombia's discipline calendars,
Thailand's discipline pages, PhilCycling's current officers and activity pages,
Malaysia's MNCF/UCI evidence, and the Indonesian and Hong Kong official sport
profiles support the other cycling mappings.

Athletics maps to road running and track and field. Argentina also maps to trail
running, supported by CADA's current national ultra-trail coverage. Duathlon is
included for Chile through FECHITRI's 2026 international age-group call-up,
Malaysia through its explicit governing-body remit, and Hong Kong through its
2026 federation event programme. Other new triathlon entries remain triathlon-only;
World Triathlon's global navigation is not evidence of a particular federation's
discipline coverage. These mappings are reviewed coverage, not exclusions from
all possible responsibilities.

## Names and destination findings

- CADA's official website is `cada-atletismo.com`, confirmed by its own site
  relaunch notice and the continental `.com` directory. The similarly named
  `.org` website states that it is independently maintained; it is not used.
- Philippine athletics uses `philippineathletics.org`, whose current about page
  identifies PATAFA. The older `philathletics.org` destination listed by Asian
  Athletics redirected to unrelated gambling content and is excluded.
- Colombia's `fecodatle.com` homepage returned 404; `fecodatle.org` contains
  unrelated material. The public destination is the Colombian Olympic
  Committee's FECODATLE member profile.
- Akuatik Indonesia's own history documents its August 2023 change from PRSI.
  Thailand's current site uses Thailand Aquatic Sports Association (TASA).
  World Aquatics retains older names for both; aliases preserve discovery.
- Philippine Aquatics, Inc. is the current World Aquatics member. The link opens
  its working English site, not its separate old construction landing page.
- Malaysia's cycling destination is `mncf.my`, as linked by the Olympic Council
  of Malaysia. The older `.org.my` domain is not used.
- Seven new entries use explicitly labelled official member-directory profiles:
  Argentine, Colombian, Thai, Philippine and Indonesian triathlon; Colombian
  athletics; and Indonesian cycling. Argentina's World Triathlon entry lists a
  social channel, Thailand's has no standalone website, Colombia's triathlon
  site was under maintenance, two triathlon sites were unavailable, and `icf.id`
  was under construction. These are destination limitations, not doubts about
  the bodies' existence or affiliation.
- Hong Kong is a separate sporting jurisdiction and is counted as a territory.
  Its canonical country-filter value remains `Hong Kong`, matching the site's
  existing country mapping. Federation names retain Hong Kong, China wording.

## Validation

The directory verifier checks all record references and relationships, existing
territory rules, eight new country filters, Argentina's separate cycling remits,
renamed-body aliases and the corrected official destinations. Before publication,
the original 194 records were compared structurally against the expanded data.
Type checking and the development build validate integration without invoking
local catalogue publication or database migration scripts. Public checks cover
new profiles, country/sport filters, member-directory labels and structured data,
and sitemap inclusion after deployment.

This is a directory expansion, not a global census, a reassessment of every older
record, or permission to collect personal athlete/result data from these sites.
