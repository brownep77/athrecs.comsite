# Directory expansion: Central and South America and the Dominican Republic

Reviewed on 23 October 2026. Adds 32 national federations, taking the directory
from 293 to 325 organisations, including 277 national bodies across 69 countries
and territories. All 293 preceding records and review dates are unchanged.

Every country also gains swimming and track-and-field coverage. Road running is
included for Costa Rica, Paraguay and Peru. The other athletics entries remain
track-and-field-only in this batch. These sport filters describe the evidence
reviewed, not every discipline a federation may administer.

## Identity and discipline evidence

- Current World Triathlon profiles list all eight national triathlon bodies as
  affiliated. Costa Rica's 2026 federation duathlon licence and Guatemala's own
  September 2026 duathlon event page establish the two duathlon mappings. Global
  World Triathlon navigation is not used as evidence of a national duathlon remit.
- World Aquatics' national-federation directory establishes swimming identities.
  Federation sites, Guatemala's CDAG, the Dominican Olympic Committee and Peru's
  national sports institute help resolve newer names and domains.
- NACAC lists the Costa Rican, Dominican and Guatemalan athletics federations.
  Atletismo Sudamericano's `.com` member directory identifies Bolivia, Panama,
  Paraguay and Peru; Ecuador's own site identifies its federation. Guatemala's
  NACAC-listed older domain redirects to `fdna.gt`.
- Costa Rica's cycling site, Ecuador's regulation sections, Guatemala's home page
  and Peru's discipline navigation explicitly cover road, track, mountain biking
  and BMX. Paraguay's federation institutional page on Google Sites describes
  those same disciplines; the identity and contact details are corroborated by
  COPACI and Paraguay's national sports secretariat.
- Bolivia's road remit is supported by COPACI's national road-championship report
  of 28 March 2022, alongside current federation identity checks. This historical
  report is not presented as a new 2026 competition. The Dominican Olympic
  Committee's 2026 Vuelta Independencia announcement establishes FEDOCI's road
  programme. The UCI's indexed 2026 National Road Championships - Panama entry
  corroborates FEPACI's road programme; direct retrieval returned a navigation shell.
- Costa Rica's athletics site has a road calendar. Paraguay's site publishes
  national 10 km and marathon information. Peru's 2026 federation calendar PDF,
  downloaded and visually inspected on pages 2 and 3, lists national marathon
  and half-marathon championships. No athlete performances are imported.

## Names and destinations

- The Dominican swimming federation changed from FEDONA to **Federación Dominicana
  de Deportes Acuáticos RD (FEDDA)**. The Olympic Committee's 5 March 2025 rename
  announcement confirms the change; the old domain redirects to `feddard.org`.
  Both names remain searchable.
- Peru's current aquatics site identifies **Federación Deportiva Nacional de
  Deportes Acuáticos (FDNDA)**. The national sports institute's September 2026
  resolution corroborates that name. World Aquatics still uses the older swimming
  name, retained as an alias. Its listed `fdpn.org` website remains the destination.
- Paraguay swimming uses the working `fepada.org` site with 2026 federation
  notices, replacing the unavailable `fepana.org.py` address still listed by
  World Aquatics. FEPANA and the former natación name remain searchable.
- Bolivia swimming retains the name used by its Olympic Committee and the World
  Aquatics identity listing. The aquatics name visible on the social page reached
  through its older website is included as an alias. The destination is the
  labelled World Aquatics member directory.
- Ecuador cycling uses the current `fec.ec` website and regulations. Guatemala
  swimming uses `fenadegua.gt`, corroborated by CDAG. Panama swimming uses the
  working `fpnpty.com` website listed by World Aquatics; the different older
  Olympic Committee-linked address was unavailable.
- Peru triathlon uses the working non-`www` address `fedenatri.pe`; the `www`
  address returned an error. Current federation content confirms the national role.
- Costa Rica swimming's listed website was blocked to automated retrieval with
  HTTP 406. Its official indexed content and the World Aquatics listing agree on
  identity and domain. The website is retained; a blocked request is not treated
  as proof that the public site is dead.
- Panama cycling's current standalone site mixed federation material with unrelated
  gambling content. It is excluded as a public destination and source reference.
  The Olympic Committee's federation directory is used instead, with road coverage
  independently supported by the UCI. Other cycling disciplines are not mapped
  from the excluded page.
- Ten additions use labelled official member directories: Bolivia cycling,
  swimming and triathlon; Dominican athletics, cycling and triathlon; Ecuador
  triathlon; Panama athletics and cycling; Paraguay cycling. Unavailable standalone
  addresses and social-only listings are not silently presented as verified national
  websites. The existing member-directory structured-data treatment is preserved.

## Validation

The directory verifier covers the eight new country filters, discipline mappings,
duathlon exclusions, renamed aquatics aliases and corrected destinations. Structural
comparison checks that the preceding 293 records are unchanged. Type checking and
the development build validate integration without local database migrations.
Live checks cover the 32 new profiles, review dates, external links, member-directory
labels and structured data, country/sport/search filters and sitemap inclusion.

This is a researched addition, not a complete census or a fresh audit of older entries.
