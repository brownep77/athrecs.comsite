## Prevention

The collector now makes every missing candidate start explicit in its saved
research gaps even if the research model omitted it. Its research instructions
require following linked schedules, FAQs, race instructions/PDFs and authorised
entry programmes and distinguishing unavailable, inaccessible, conflicting and
unchecked times. This exposes missing work; it does not verify the whole catalogue.

The existing daily fixture monitoring task must work imminent missing starts
first (48 hours, seven days, 30 days), retain per-edition check outcomes and
remaining counts, and flag unresolved seven-day coverage as actionable work.
Keep distant events visible when organisers have not published a schedule; never
invent a time to satisfy completeness. Check the public page after publication.
