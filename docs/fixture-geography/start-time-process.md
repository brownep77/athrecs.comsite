# Required start-time research within three months

Owner instruction, 25 October 2026: make every reasonable effort to obtain a
verified start for every race within the next three months. This applies to all
sports and countries on AthRecs, existing listings and new imports, and each
separately advertised distance, category, wave or session.

## Rolling scope and ownership

At the beginning of each daily 08:00 Europe/London fixture run, calculate the
inclusive window from the run date to that date plus three calendar months.
Clamp month-end to the last valid day; do not substitute 90 days or a quarter.
On 25 October 2026 the window is 25 October 2026–25 January 2027. Race dates and
clock times remain local to the venue; check the venue date near day boundaries.

The existing **AthRecs fixtures, results and athletes** automation owns the
catalogue-wide sweep. Other collectors perform this review for their own
in-window candidates and pass unresolved editions into the same checkpoint;
they must not create parallel schedules or duplicate editions.

Query the fresh live catalogue and existing checkpoint on every run. Include
empty, placeholder, malformed, provisional and conflicting starts, plus stored
times without current-edition evidence. Reconcile checked presentation overrides,
cancelled/withheld editions and genuine duplicate placeholders before reporting
public gaps. Count events and race-distance editions separately. Do not hide a
legitimate race because the organiser has not yet released its timetable.

Work today/next two days first, then seven days, 30 days, and the rest of the
three-month window. Within each band prioritise unchecked and overdue editions;
rotate sports/countries and reserve capacity for untouched editions. Group
distances sharing an official programme while checking each one's actual start.
Checkpoint between batches and report capacity shortfalls rather than declaring
the window checked when only a sample was processed.

## Mandatory evidence checklist

For every in-window edition, inspect reasonably available sources in this order:

1. The current organiser event page and linked race-day timetable/schedule.
2. FAQs, race instructions, athlete guides and linked PDFs. Inspect images or
   scanned pages when extracted text is incomplete.
3. The organiser-authorised entry programme and distance/category tabs.
4. The relevant governing-body fixture or championship programme.
5. Official organiser news and official social updates for later changes.
6. Exact event + year + distance searches in English and the relevant local
   language. Secondary calendars are discovery leads; open the primary evidence.

Follow links beyond the landing page. A snippet, working URL, one missing field
or one failed page is not a completed search. For blocked pages, try linked
official documents, an accessible official alternative or authorised entry page.
Respect access controls and rate limits. Record unavailable source types and
why they could not be checked; never describe an unvisited source as inspected.

Match year, date, distance, adult/junior category and wave/session. Distinguish
registration, village opening, shuttle departures and packet collection from
the actual start. Prefer the latest edition-specific organiser timetable over
a generic summary; preserve conflicting evidence and the reason for a decision.
Never copy another year's time, infer a time, or substitute midnight for unknown.

## Required review record and follow-up

Keep a record per canonical edition ID in the existing private checkpoint:
event/edition ID, date, distance/category, status, attempted URL + outcome +
checked timestamp, evidence excerpt/paraphrase, local start and IANA timezone
when known, wave/window scope, previous value, next action and next check date.

Use **confirmed**, **organiser-not-yet-published**, **inaccessible**,
**conflicting**, or **unchecked**. A partially completed checklist stays
unchecked with its completed attempts retained. Only a completed source search
can support not-yet-published. Confirmed means source-reviewed, not merely that
the database contains a plausible HH:mm. Preserve a confirmed value while
investigating a later conflict; flag it explicitly rather than erasing it.

| Time to race                     | Retry unresolved / unverified starts        |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 31 days to three calendar months | At least weekly                             |
| 8–30 days                        | At least every three days                   |
| Today through seven days         | Every daily run; urgent if still unresolved |

Do the full initial review as soon as a race enters the window. Recheck confirmed
starts at seven and two days, and revisit any new official change immediately
when discovered. A new race or changed date in the window is due now. Bound
retries by race day; do not retry a stale URL indefinitely while leaving other
editions unchecked. After race day retain evidence in the historical record.

If research is exhausted and contact is needed, retain the verified organiser
contact route and a draft enquiry as the next action. Sending email, messages or
contact forms requires explicit messaging authority; this process does not send
them automatically.

## Publication and completion gate

Publish source-reviewed corrections through the existing staged, validated,
audited workflow, preserving canonical IDs and checking concurrent work. Record
before/after values, source, date checked and exact distance/wave scope. Verify
database readback and the live page including local timezone and calendar text.
Keep the public unknown-start label while research remains unresolved.

A collection batch may succeed while its time research remains incomplete.
Keep those gaps open. Every run records counts by the four urgency bands,
separating confirmed, researched-unresolved and unchecked; list urgent editions,
overdue retries, blockers and the next cursor. Never claim complete coverage
until every in-window edition has a current review record. Notify Paul of
meaningful progress, new urgent gaps or an actionable capacity/access blocker.

The collector research prompt includes the checklist and exact rolling dates.
Its parser retains empty and invalid starts in the gap report, prioritising
in-window gaps even when the report is capped. This detects unfinished work;
it does not prove that an external source was actually read. Human/source review
and the scheduled checkpoint remain required.
