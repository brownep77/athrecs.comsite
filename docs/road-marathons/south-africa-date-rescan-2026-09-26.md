The sponsor's Facebook post independently repeats it. Neither source establishes current entry availability.
- [Elands organiser profile](https://www.instagram.com/tracn4_elands_marathon/): actual profile HTML inspected; its public biography states 27 February 2027 and entries opening TBA. The exact account is linked by the official Elands website. The bio-change publication date was unavailable. An indexed sponsor entry-opening lead was not treated as verified.

- [Hillcrest's Racepass page](https://racepass.com/za/races/the-hillcrest-marathon) gives 7 February in the headline and marathon option. The attached flyer was visually checked and belongs to 8 February 2026. RunningCalendar's alternative 14 February 2027 is expressly estimated.
- Soweto follow-up after the user's challenge: [Gauteng Tourism](https://visit.gauteng.net/events/african-bank-soweto-marathon-2026) and its [full event article](https://www.gauteng.net/events/african-bank-soweto-marathon-2026-2/) explicitly advertise Sunday 29 November 2026. This is enough to show an advertised date prominently; describing it only as a conflict was less helpful than the available evidence allows. Start times disagree (06:00 versus 05:30), so none is added.
- The qualification is retained because [Africa Daily's direct CGA interview of 18 August](https://africadaily.co.za/who-is-advertising-the-soweto-marathon-cga-says-2026-race-is-not-sanctioned/) specifically addresses the advertised 29 November race and says sanction had not been granted at that point. Some social feeds, original posts and organiser sites were unavailable. Not finding a confirmed date is not proof that no announcement exists. Blocked pages and indexed-only leads were not treated as inspected confirmations.
