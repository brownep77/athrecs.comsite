Selwyn, Sasol Secunda and Infantry School Cango remain Unknown because no reliable full-marathon-only count could be established from the accessible results and reports.

Field notes identify the edition, measure and any secondary report used. These additions use a completed 2025 or 2026 edition as a rough indication of scale, not a multi-year average. They distinguish entrants from finishers and exclude capacity figures and combined festival totals. The country tables and individual race pages show Unknown when no reliable estimate is available; they never substitute an official-results link for the field size.

Country tables now have a separate geographic column, with the same detail on race cards, undated listings and individual race pages. Labels are County / area for the UK, County for Ireland, State / district for the USA, State / territory for Australia, Province for Canada and South Africa, and Region for New Zealand. UK nations remain separate from counties and council areas. Event addressRegion and page descriptions use the same area values.

All 131 race introductions, seven country introductions and the running hub use a clear enthusiast voice with restrained humour. Public explanations of automatic date expiry and editorial processing were removed; date filtering still runs in the background. A public TSX scan found no literal AI-written/generated/powered labels; staff tools and code comments remain functional.

- Australia: Canberra Marathon, Townsville Airport Marathon, Perth Running Festival Marathon, Adelaide Marathon, Orange Running Festival Marathon, Busselton Bay Run Marathon, Alice Springs Marathon.

Some other races have overall/elite summaries but only partial age-group coverage. Unknown future dates are not inferred. Browser and text-source attempts encountered unavailable or blocked results on several official timing services.

## Specific source distinctions

- Edinburgh 2026 uses the confirmed full-marathon event=1060 results. Its female 40F filter did not provide a category-first row; no winner was invented. Category codes were matched to rows rather than trusting the filter label alone.
- Windermere 2026 summaries cover all 22 populated categories exposed by the timer, preserving its Male, Female and Other grouping. Online summary standings may differ from race-day prize presentations.
- Dublin 2025 wheelchair and VI records preserve published positions and separate finish/chip times; equal times are not assumed to mean a tie.
- Ottawa 2026 fastest-Canadian performances are not labelled national championship wins. The linked federation report did not independently establish the reported women’s performance as a win.
- Disney 2026 had a weather-modified course; its caution is retained and its field total is not used.
- Edmonton 2026 organiser course-measurement information is retained. Do not silently present affected performances as standard-distance records.
- Hamilton/Mississauga field sizes exclude unfinished rows. Other approximate result-count figures identify their basis and do not masquerade as exact registered-entry averages.
- Los Angeles uses its 2025 field, avoiding the 2026 optional mile-18 finish. Whanganui uses the full-marathon run division and explicitly excludes the separate marathon walk. Rimouski includes both full-distance marathon divisions, including Pace du Bonheur.
- Quebec City's 2025 secondary summaries differ by around 5%; the displayed estimate of about 2,200 is deliberately approximate and attributes its selected count to FindMyMarathon. Ballarat and Adelaide source-count differences are small enough not to affect their rounded estimates.
- St. George 2027 is explicitly listed in the organiser's indexed future-date schedule. The older registration URL could not be opened following the site migration; its expanded indexed content was inspected. It was not inferred from an annual pattern.
- Steamtown and Seattle were excluded from the US expansion because their current courses could not be confidently classified as fully paved. Erie and Tucson were used instead.
- Little Rock past-edition links use the organiser's stable results archive, replacing its unavailable short-domain redirect. Its 2025 and 2026 division winners come from the organiser's winners table.
- South Africa covers standard-distance road events, excluding ultras such as Comrades and Two Oceans. Kaapsehoop is classified as a road race; an older account describes an unpaved turnaround, so the guide does not promise uninterrupted asphalt. Hillcrest also has a short linking path and sports-ground finish.
- Cape Town's 2025 marathon was cancelled; its archives cover 2026 and 2024. The 2026 AbbottWMM age-group World Championships are labelled separately from the main race. Winelands uses the current Coetzenburg venue and provisional route, avoiding an outdated route PDF.
- Capital City replaces the inactive Maritzburg City candidate. Its current homepage/flyer supplies the 2027 date; a contradictory old page and an incorrectly linked Durban route/results archive are excluded. Durban City's confirmed Racepass date takes precedence over a provisional directory date.
- Peninsula uses the later federation workbook consistently rather than mixing it with different live-timing values. NMB 2024 uses full-results HMS times and category codes, explicitly separate from a prize sheet with different times and award bands. NMB 2025 has 1,190 inspected timed full-marathon rows and 19 category leaders.
- Johnson Crane, Pretoria, Johannesburg City, Vaal and Dolphin Coast preserve source gun/chip distinctions and identify timing-table category leaders rather than assuming prize eligibility. Source notes about age tags remain visible where relevant. Elands 2026 and Buffs 2026 retain the provisional status in the reports inspected.
- Nine South African future dates are now confirmed, including Johnson Crane and Elands added after a deeper social-announcement scan. Ten remaining races have internal date-research notes; all unconfirmed dates display as TBC under the user's latest instruction. Estimates and the Buffs weekend are excluded from the confirmed calendar and SportsEvent markup. See [the detailed date recheck](south-africa-date-rescan-2026-09-26.md). All 20 races have entry, route, media and official results links; historical category coverage varies with accessible evidence.
