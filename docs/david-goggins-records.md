## Representation

One public-figure profile, without private contact data, date of birth, photo, claimed ownership or claimed current club affiliation. The database assigns the normal permanent athlete number. The internal unattached club is only a schema fallback, not a verified affiliation.

Distances preserve the nominal result category, converted to km for the existing schema. They are not certified measurements. In particular, Bigfoot 2025 has reports of a revised course; 200 Mile is the source category, not a claim to an exact 200 miles. Moab course measurements vary by edition. The biography and edition notes explain this. No standard road PB is inferred from any trail performance. Trail and mixed-surface rows remain ineligible for the road PB strip. Badwater and Strolling Jim are road ultras. DNFs and DNS entries have no finish time or placing and do not count as finishes or achievements. Timed-distance performances are shown in a separate profile section, with no invented finish time or precise date; they do not enter result totals, finish achievements or road PB calculations. Triathlon, cycling, pull-ups and training challenges are outside this running batch.
