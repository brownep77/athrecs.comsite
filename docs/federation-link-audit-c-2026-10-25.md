# Federation link audit C — 25 October 2026

This pass checks all 66 direct road-cycling-tagged organisation URLs, excluding cross-sport funding/Olympic bodies and membership-directory references. It also follows 25 detail/reference destinations. Earlier aquatics and triathlon audits remain unchanged.

## Findings

Forty-eight homepages returned organisation-related content. Nine returned partial content, six could not be retrieved, two need content review and one primarily exposed historical material. These are content-screening outcomes, not independent verification of every organisation fact or downstream data record. The JSON records the URL, resolved URL, title, status and qualification for each organisation.

British Cycling, Cycling South Africa, Portugal, Brazil, Latvia and Hong Kong failed in both retrieval methods. A blocked request or timeout does not establish that a domain is wrong. Their URLs are retained with explicit limitations. UCI, Ireland, Japan, Norway, India, Lithuania, Slovenia, Bulgaria and Israel returned limited material; a calendar shell or zero loaded events does not prove the absence of competitions.

German Cycling's homepage contains placeholder text, but its imprint and the opened UEC membership page corroborate the domain. The linked rad-net homepage contains dated cycling news; it has not been promoted to a verified results feed. Jamaica's homepage includes placeholder text and past events under an upcoming heading. Its track championship page has a two-day title, a single-day date field and a CAC Games report body. That source is held for review; neither dates nor midnight values are imported as race starts.

Trinidad and Tobago's opened track and road indexes contain 2017–2021 material. Its public directory detail now explains that these are historical references rather than a confirmed current fixture calendar. The homepage remains unchanged; no replacement current site was established. Existing references and original source check notes are preserved.

## Deeper sources

Scottish Cycling provides a provisional calendar, national-record document links and a championship page linking a 2025 results book. The calendar's text and spreadsheet filenames differ in update dates, and the winter calendar is explicitly draft. Document links were inspected as links; PDF rows and spreadsheet contents were not compared. A current page URL does not make the championship results a 2026 edition.

Cycling Canada and Swiss Cycling expose named event tables, results indexes and athlete directories. Those are useful research routes, but team membership does not establish nationality and results indexes do not verify individual performances. Swiss Cycling's homepage results teaser includes a future date, reinforcing the need to check each completed edition. Beicio Cymru's event index returned only descriptive text; a linked Newport Omnium page did expose event, venue, session and category details. A session window is not the start of every race.

## Registry and validation

Nineteen disabled/manual sources were added after exact-URL and source-ID duplicate checks. Sixty-seven existing source records receive dated observations, preserving their original notes. The registry grows from 959 to 978 sources, with 36 enabled and 942 disabled. Each new source is explicitly mapped to its organisation. Only the Trinidad and Tobago cycling directory record changes; no fixture, athlete, nationality or result data is changed.

Full captures and before/after registry records are retained in the private staff audit. Required validation covers source schema and organisation coverage, collection counts, preservation of existing records, TypeScript, focused lint and a development build. Live checks follow deployment. Registration alone does not establish collection rights or enable automated collection.
