# Mo Farah road history — checked 17 September 2026

The standard athlete profile now uses 52 sourced road entries: 50 finishes and two DNFs, covering 2004–2023. They feed the same results table, filters, PBs, podiums and achievements used by other athletes. The profile remains an independent, unclaimed editorial profile.

## Coverage and sources

World Athletics' annual results were checked for every available year from 1997 to 2023. No road rows appeared before 2004. Organiser and contemporary reports fill confirmed omissions, including the 2006 Finn Valley 5K, 2009 road mile and Great South Run, and 2023 Manchester and Great North Run. This is source-checked coverage, not a claim that the archives contain every race of his life.

Each result below links to its evidence. World Athletics athlete links refer to the corresponding year in Statistics → Results. Edition-specific official results are preferred where available.

## Interpretation and discrepancies

- Great North Run editions in this import are assisted. They count as finishes and wins but cannot replace his eligible half-marathon PB of 59:32 in Lisbon. The course rule is edition-specific, not inferred for future editions.
- World Athletics marks the 2018 and 2019 Big Half results UNC. They count as finishes but are excluded from PB comparisons.
- London 2013 was a planned partial run; London 2020 was a pacing appearance. Both retain the published DNF status and have no finish time, so neither contributes a finish, marathon or Major completion.
- Great North Run 2018 is 59:27 in the organiser's final results. Some preliminary reports say 59:26; the final official result is retained.
- NYC Half 2011 uses NYRR's official history, 1:00:24, in preference to the World Athletics annual table's 1:00:23.
- London 10,000 2022 retains World Athletics' 28:46. Contemporary reports and Power of 10 list 28:44; this disagreement is preserved in the edition note. Neither changes a PB.
- Djibouti 2021 retains World Athletics' 1:03:07 rather than Power of 10's 1:03:06. Nike London 2004 retains World Athletics' 28:58 rather than Power of 10's 28:59. The source is explicit.
- The 2008 road mile is listed by World Athletics as hand-timed 4:12.1h, represented as 4:13 at whole-second road precision, with the original value retained in the edition note.
- Power of 10 lists a two-mile road best of 8:38 in 2010. Its exact race date was not established; it appears as an additional sourced best, never as an invented dated result.
- 15K 42:03 (Cardiff, 26 March 2016) and 20K 56:27 (Lisbon, 22 March 2015) are intermediate splits, shown separately without extra finish/achievement counts.

## Release safeguards

Only the public-figure refresh marker advances. The global catalogue marker and permanent URL protections remain unchanged. Existing canonical Great North Run and Manchester event slugs are reused. The real database regression verifies counts, null DNF times, idempotence and the preserved retired-URL guard. Unit checks verify six eligible road PB categories, 50 finishes, six marathon finishes and two Major locations. Browser checks verify the shared PB/results/achievements layout, assisted labels, filters, pagination, the loaded photo and mobile width.
