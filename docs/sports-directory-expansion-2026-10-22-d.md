# Directory expansion: eight further national jurisdictions

Reviewed on 22 October 2026. Adds 32 national federations, taking the directory
from 229 to 261 organisations, including 213 national bodies across 53 countries
and territories. All 229 earlier records are preserved unchanged.

Each country also has a swimming federation and an athletics federation mapped
to road running and track and field. These are reviewed directory mappings, not
an assertion that the bodies have no other responsibilities.

## Primary evidence

Each entry retains official references and its review date. World Triathlon's
current member profiles establish all eight triathlon affiliations. World
Aquatics' national-federation directory identifies all eight swimming bodies.
UEC's individual national member profiles establish all eight cycling identities
and their websites; national sites establish the discipline mappings.

Balkan Athletics' member list identifies the Croatian, Slovenian, Serbian and
Bulgarian athletics bodies. Slovakia's federation contact page, Iceland's current
federation site, Luxembourg's national Olympic committee profile and Israel's
federation about page provide the other athletics references.

Slovenia, Slovakia and Bulgaria explicitly list road, mountain biking, track and
BMX on their national sites. Croatia and Serbia provide current road and mountain
bike activity. Iceland's federation calendar identifies mountain-bike events
alongside its road programme. Luxembourg's federation publishes its national
junior team's participation in the 2026 track world championships; its site also
covers road and mountain biking. Israel's site covers road, mountain biking and
track records. BMX is not automatically inferred from UCI membership.

Duathlon evidence comes from Croatia's official 2026 national supersprint event
notice, Slovenia's federation calendar, Slovakia's call for 2026 championship
organisers, Serbia's current federation programme, Bulgaria's national competition
rules and Luxembourg's federation event listings. Iceland and Israel remain
triathlon-only in this batch because duathlon coverage was not independently
established. World Triathlon's shared navigation is not used as local remit evidence.

## Names and destinations

- Local-language names and English/acronym aliases support search. Icelandic
  names also have ASCII aliases for letters not covered by accent normalization.
- Bulgaria's current World Triathlon member is Bulgarian Triathlon Association,
  using `bat.triathlon.bg`. Its member-profile URL retains an older federation
  label; the display name follows the current member entry and national site.
- Serbia's triathlon site uses Srpski triatlon. The World Triathlon profile's
  `serbian-triathon-union` spelling is retained exactly as its working URL.
- World Aquatics lists Croatia Swimming Association; the record retains the
  national site's Hrvatski plivački savez name and both English aliases.
- All 32 destinations are national-body websites. Existing labelled member-directory
  fallbacks remain unchanged. Slovak triathlon and Serbian swimming blocked one
  automated retrieval method but were accessible through the web retrieval service.
  Luxembourg athletics blocked direct automated retrieval; its official-domain
  indexed content and national Olympic committee profile confirm the destination.
  These limitations are recorded without treating a blocked request as a dead site.
- Swimming summaries do not imply that every aquatic discipline is administered
  by the same body; World Aquatics may list separate national water-polo or artistic
  swimming organisations.

## Validation

The directory verifier checks the eight new country filters, all four federation
groups, discipline-specific cycling and duathlon mappings, translated-name
searches, references and relationships. Structural comparison confirms the 229
previous records are unchanged. Type checking and the development build validate
integration without local database migrations or catalogue publication.
Post-deployment checks cover all 32 profiles, country/sport filters, existing
member-directory labels, Northern Ireland remit and sitemap inclusion.

This addition does not claim complete global coverage or reassess every older
record. Official sources establish organisational roles, not permission to import
athlete profiles or results.
