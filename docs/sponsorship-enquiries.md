# Sponsorship enquiries

RunRecs `/sponsorship` introduces race sponsorship. AthRecs `/brands` now leads
with brand exposure through athletes and influencers, with creator intake at
`/sponsorship?kind=creator`. RunRecs links from its homepage and site footer.

## What works

- Verified account holders aged 18+ can submit a brand, race organiser or creator brief.
- Private enquiries are stored in the shared database, with the originating site derived on the server.
- Submitters see only their own enquiries, status and team response; they can withdraw open enquiries.
- Staff review both sites at `https://update.athrecs.com/admin/sponsorship` using the existing host and Google allowlist gate.
- Staff responses stay in the account dashboard. The feature sends no external messages or introductions.
- Review updates check the revision and cannot reopen withdrawn or closed enquiries. Actions are audited.
- Duplicate request IDs are idempotent. Submission caps are serialized per user: five open enquiries, ten per day.

This is an enquiry service. It does not publish race sponsorship listings,
establish organiser ownership of catalogue records, verify creators automatically,
make contracts, or handle sponsorship payments. Existing athlete claim checks and
partnership opt-ins still apply to direct opportunity applications.

## Commercial model proposed for discussion

A disclosed success fee can be agreed separately for introductions that result in
a paid sponsorship. A possible starting proposal is 10% of cash sponsorship
actually received, excluding VAT and separately agreed pass-through costs. It is
a proposal, not a current site tariff or an asserted industry standard.

Before charging, define the contracting legal entity, who pays, the fee base,
payment timing, cancellations/refunds, instalments, renewals and any pre-existing
relationship exceptions. Agree rights, deliverables, exclusivity and advertising
disclosures. Product-only support needs its own valuation or fixed-fee agreement.
Initially sponsors can pay the recipient directly, with the platform invoicing
its agreed fee separately. Integrated split payments require a suitable marketplace
payment provider and review of the actual UK regulatory and tax setup.
If the service expands into negotiating as an athlete representative, check the
applicable governing-body licensing rules and the athlete's existing agent agreements.

No commission percentage or automatic charge is enabled in this release.
